MULTI-LABEL SAFETY RULE
-----------------------
This is a multi-label dataset.
Augmentation must never change the patient disease labels.
Examples:
- AS + AR must remain AS + AR after augmentation.
- AS + AR + MR + MS must remain AS + AR + MR + MS after augmentation.
- No augmented recording may become AS-only or AR-only.
- The special None case is not a normal disease label and is excluded from augmentation logic.

AUGMENTATION POLICY
-------------------
Goal:
- improve minority-label and rare-combination representation
- avoid excessive duplication
- keep patient count fixed at 76
- increase only the training recording count

Prioritized groups:
1. AS single-disease patients
2. rare AS-containing combinations
3. AR and other low-count combinations
4. extremely rare combinations

Do not heavily augment the already common MR and MS groups.
Use moderate augmentation ratios.

ALLOWED OPERATIONS
------------------
- small gain/amplitude scaling
- small circular time shifting
- very mild time stretching
- subtle background-noise addition only if needed

Not allowed:
- pitch shifting
- aggressive time stretching
- extreme amplitude changes
- unrealistic synthetic heart-sound artifacts
